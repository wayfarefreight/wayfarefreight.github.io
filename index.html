01 / WHO WE ARE

A hands-on partner for the journey ahead.

WayFare Freight is a Singapore freight forwarder with a knowledgeable, experienced team and a global network spanning continents. We provide freight solutions tailored to the requirements of each client and shipment.

WayFare Freight coordinates FCL, LCL, door-to-door shipping, customs clearance, warehousing and distribution.

01

Full container load (FCL)

An entire container is reserved for your cargo. It stays separate from other shipments, with less handling during transit and added security.

02

Less than container load (LCL)

Smaller shipments are consolidated with other cargo. Shared container space offers flexibility and can reduce shipping costs.

OCEAN CONTAINERS

Choosing the right container.

Shipping and storage containers come in several sizes. Standard 20 ft and 40 ft containers are 8 ft wide externally and usually 8 ft 6 in high. A high-cube container adds another foot of height, reaching 9 ft 6 in. Smaller new-build containers may be suited to tight spaces and restricted access.

Equipment for specialist air cargo.

WayFare Freight’s guide shows six air-container categories. Many containers work with more than one aircraft, but compatibility varies. For example, its refrigerated RZX container fits only the main deck of a Boeing 747F, while an RKN can be loaded on 19 aircraft types.
