01 / WHO WE ARE

A hands-on partner for the journey ahead.

WayFare Freight is a Singapore freight forwarder with an experienced team and a global network. We make international transportation easier to manage with practical advice, dependable handling and solutions tailored to each shipment.

Our team works across sea freight, air freight, trucking, warehousing, full and less than container loads, awkward cargo and roll-on/roll-off shipments. We help navigate customs regulations, documentation and international shipping requirements while paying close attention to the safety of your goods.
